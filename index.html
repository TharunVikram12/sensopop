SensoPop

A bubble world built for your senses.

Pop, listen, and feel your way through a calm, customisable bubble environment — designed for sensory exploration, focus, and gentle attention play.

Choose your senses

Set how many bubbles appear, how fast they move, and how strong the sound and vibration should feel — before you begin.

Pop, listen, feel

Every bubble bursts with its own sound and haptic pulse. Play freely, or try a focused activity like colour matching or memory sequences.

See your patterns

A private dashboard shows which colours, sounds, and speeds you tend to enjoy — so future sessions can gently adapt to you.

What SensoPop is. SensoPop is an experimental, educational sensory-engagement prototype. It supports sensory exploration and attention-based play through customisable visual, sound, and vibration feedback.

What it isn't. It is not a medical, diagnostic, or therapeutic tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or cure ADHD, autism, or any other condition, and it does not label or assess any user. If you have questions about attention, sensory processing, or development, please speak with a qualified professional.

Set up your session

Adjust visuals, sound, and vibration until it feels right. You can change these anytime.

🔊 Sound

Soundscape gives every colour its own themed sound — rain, birdsong, a sunny chime, marimba, a soft drum, and a magical galaxy chime. Simple tones and soft pops are quieter, more uniform alternatives.

📳 Vibration

Haptics require a supported device and browser (mostly Android + Chrome). If unsupported, this is skipped automatically.

🧩 Mode

Gently nudges future defaults based on your own past sessions. You can always override it manually.
